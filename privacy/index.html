Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

M2Agency Inc. ("we", "us", "Global Travel Center") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use world-entry.com and our travel authorization application services.

1. Information we collect

To process your visa or electronic travel authorization application, we collect:

  • Personal identification: full name, date of birth, gender, nationality, place of birth
  • Passport information: passport number, issuing country, dates of issue and expiry
  • Travel information: destination, planned arrival date, address at destination, purpose of travel
  • Contact information: email address, phone number
  • Eligibility responses: answers to government-required eligibility questions
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe, Inc.; we do not store full card details
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information (via cookies and analytics)

2. How we use your information

  • Submit your application to the relevant government authority on your behalf
  • Communicate application status, approval, or rejection
  • Provide customer support
  • Process payments and issue refunds where applicable
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud
  • Improve our services (aggregated, non-identifiable data only)

3. Information sharing

We share your information only as necessary to provide our services:

  • Government authorities: U.S. CBP, Korean Ministry of Justice, UK Home Office, Australian Department of Home Affairs — for the sole purpose of submitting your application
  • Payment processor: Stripe, Inc. — for secure payment processing
  • Service providers: hosting (GitHub Pages), email (M2Agency Inc. servers), analytics — under strict confidentiality obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

4. Data security

We use SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission and follow industry-standard security practices. Payment details are handled exclusively by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor.

5. Data retention

We retain application data for as long as legally required and to provide ongoing customer support. Typically, application data is retained for 5 years from the date of submission.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@m2-agency.net or via the contact form.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the Site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some Site features may not function correctly.

8. International transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence, including for the purpose of submitting your application to the destination government. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the Site or email. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance.
