Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

M2Agency Inc. ("we", "us", "our") operates world-entry.com ("the Site", "Global Travel Center"), an independent information guide about electronic travel authorizations and visa-free travel. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information is processed when you visit the Site and how it is used.

1. What we do not collect

The Site does not offer application forms and does not process applications. We do not collect passport details, dates of birth, travel itineraries, eligibility answers or payment information, and we do not transmit any personal data to government authorities. No payment processor is used on the Site.

2. Information we process

  • Server logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the Site, standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages requested, the referring page and the date and time of access may be recorded in server logs by the hosting provider, in accordance with its own privacy statement.
  • Cookies and analytics. We may use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to understand how the Site is used. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser information and interactions with the Site. This information is used in aggregated form only. You can prevent analytics cookies by adjusting your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. No other analytics or advertising tags are used.
  • Correspondence. If you write to us by email or post, we receive your name, contact details and the content of your message. This information is used only to respond to you.

3. How we use the information

  • To operate, secure and maintain the Site
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the Site is used and to improve its content
  • To respond to enquiries you send to us

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

4. Third parties

Personal information may be processed on our behalf by our hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) and our analytics provider (Google LLC), each under its own privacy terms. The Site links to official government websites and other third-party websites; those sites are governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

5. International transfers

Our hosting and analytics providers may store and process data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Such processing is subject to the providers' respective privacy commitments.

6. Data retention

Server log and analytics data are retained for the limited periods set by the respective providers (for Google Analytics 4, event data is retained for no longer than 14 months). Correspondence is kept only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and is deleted within one year after the matter is closed, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below.

8. Children

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
