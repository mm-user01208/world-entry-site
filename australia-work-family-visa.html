Long-Term Visas: Work and Family Streams

Separately from the short-stay visas used for tourism and short-term business, Australia offers long-term visas in work and family streams for stays of a certain length or with permanent residence in view. These visas are not grouped together simply because the stays are long; their distinctive feature is that each is designed around a clear basis — an employment relationship or a family relationship.

Unlike schemes premised on a temporary stay, many of these visas assume ongoing engagement with Australian society, and the assessment weighs the applicant's background, employment situation, family relationships, and future plans as a whole. Some also provide a pathway to permanent residence once certain conditions are met, giving the system a staged structure that links temporary stay and permanent residence.

Important Notice

Australian visas — particularly work visas and family and long-stay visas — are subject to frequent changes in rules, requirements, and administrative practice. This page is provided for general information about how the system works and does not guarantee the currency of specific requirements or their application to individual cases.

The Main Visas by Type of Applicant

Working with an Employer's Sponsorship

Australia has a well-developed set of visas premised on working with the sponsorship or nomination of a local employer. Designed to address labour shortages, they involve rigorous assessment of occupation, skills, work experience, and English language ability. Notably, "work visas" in this stream are not limited to short-term employment; some are designed with permanent residence in view.

For example, there are visas under which working in Australia for a set period, with ongoing endorsement or nomination from your employer, can lead to permanent residence. Schemes of this kind are structured in stages, envisaging a transition from temporary work to settlement, with weight placed on the nature of the work and the continuity of the employment relationship.

Family Members of Australian Citizens and Permanent Residents

Visas applied for on the basis of family relationships grant long-term stay or permanent residence on the strength of a relationship with an Australian citizen or permanent resident. They are mainly for spouses, de facto partners, and parents, and what is scrutinised closely is not simply a wish to live together but whether a genuine relationship exists.

Documentary evidence of the relationship is central to these visas, with the assessment covering the history of the relationship or marriage, cohabitation, financial ties, and other aspects from multiple angles. Processing times also tend to be long, and since these schemes are designed with permanent residence in mind, applicants should be prepared for the time and emotional commitment involved.

Other Cases Premised on Long-Term Stay or Permanent Residence

Beyond work and family relationships, visas may also exist for long-term stay or permanent residence based on particular circumstances. These include special dependency relationships, cases requiring humanitarian consideration, and schemes premised on long-term residence.

Visas of this kind are often complex in themselves, with requirements and assessment criteria that vary from case to case. General information alone is rarely enough to judge them, and specialist knowledge is often needed.

Points to Note When Considering Work and Family Stream Visas

With work and family stream long-term visas, take care not to assume that "which visa applies" can be classified simply. Judging by length of stay alone risks misunderstanding what the system is for. What matters is why you will be staying in Australia, and in what capacity.

Choosing the wrong visa, or breaching its conditions, can also affect future visa applications and the prospect of permanent residence. It is important to understand the system as a whole from the outset and decide carefully.
