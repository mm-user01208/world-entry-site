Nature of this website

Global Travel Center is an independent information guide about electronic travel authorizations and visa-free travel. The Site is free of charge: we do not sell any goods or services through it, and we do not charge any fees.

This is not a government website. We are not part of, and are not affiliated with, any government or government agency. Applications can be made only on the official government websites, which are linked from our guides. Any fees for a travel authorization are charged by, and paid directly to, the government concerned.
