Global Travel Center is not a government website. We are a private company that assists with the preparation and submission of travel visa and electronic travel authorization applications. We do not issue visas, ETAs, or any official travel documents — these are granted solely by the respective government bodies (U.S. Department of Homeland Security for ESTA, Korean Ministry of Justice for K-ETA, UK Home Office for UK ETA, Australian Department of Home Affairs for Subclass 601 ETA).

Applicants may also submit their applications directly to the relevant government portal at no service fee.
